## Suggested 12-month beginner sequence
Months 1–2 Linux/Windows; 3–4 networking; 5 Python; 6 security foundations; 7–8 Red Team and web; 9–10 Blue Team and incident response; 11 cloud/containers; 12 portfolio and specialization choice.

## Resource selection
Choose one main course, read the relevant official docs, complete labs, build an artifact, then record what you can explain independently. Verify current pricing, access and exam versions before paying.
